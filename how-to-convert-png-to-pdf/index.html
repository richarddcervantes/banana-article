How to Convert PNG to PDF

Converting PNG images into PDF format is useful when you want a document that is easier to organize, print, store, or share. The process is simple, but a few preparation steps can improve the final result.

Choose the PNG Images You Need

Start by selecting the PNG images that belong in your document. Clear screenshots, scans, graphics, receipts, and certificates can all work well. If several images belong together, place them in the order you want before conversion. Renaming files with simple page numbers can make this easier. Check each image for correct rotation and cropping so the final PDF feels organized from the first page.

Use Suitable Page Settings

Page size and orientation affect how the PDF looks. Portrait orientation works well for most scanned documents, while landscape can suit wide screenshots or charts. Common sizes such as A4 and Letter are practical for printing. Keep the original image proportions whenever possible so text and graphics remain natural. Small margins can also help the document look clean without shrinking the image too much.

Convert and Review the PDF

Open the PNGStack converter, add your PNG files, and create the PDF. After conversion, open the document and inspect every page. Check image quality, page order, margins, and orientation. Make sure important details remain visible and readable. A quick review can catch simple problems before you print, email, or archive the document.
